# AIMON: The Complete Story

This is the full story of AIMON, from the first morning in Willowbrook to the
Hall of Fame. It builds on everything that is already in the game. Chapters
1 to 9 are **built**; chapters 10 to 15 are **planned**. Where a built chapter
gets a small rewrite to set up something later, the change is listed under
**Revisions** at the end of that chapter.

It uses only the AIMON, mechanics and graphics that already exist.

---

## The heart of it

**Premise.** Long ago, VALEMORA tore open. The old texts call the wound the
**RIFT**. Where it touched, rivers ran backward and AIMON fell silent. Eight
trainers sealed it with eight great stones, the **KEYSTONES**, and became the
first **WARDENS**. Their heirs are today's GYM LEADERS. Every GYM BADGE holds a
sliver of its WARDEN's KEYSTONE.

**The villain's idea.** Each KEYSTONE hums one note. Together the eight notes
form a chord, and that chord is the seal. TEAM DISTORTION records each stone's
"song". Played **backwards**, all at once, the songs will undo the seal and
open the RIFT. Their leader, the CONDUCTOR, believes the world beyond the
RIFT is the "true" VALEMORA, where no song ever ends.

**The hero's answer.** The same chord, sung **forwards** by all eight slivers
at once, renews the seal. The BADGES were never just trophies. They are the
region's spare key. The CHAMPION'S OATH, a custom almost forgotten, asks
whoever carries all eight to renew the seal if it ever weakens. The player
starts out collecting badges for glory and ends up carrying the key to the
whole region.

**Themes.** Sound versus silence. Grief that turns into obsession (the
CONDUCTOR) and grief that turns into strength (KAI). Being first versus being
heard. Team Distortion's motto is *"Bend. Break. Become."* The WARDENS answer:
*"Hold. Heal. Harmonize."*

The gaps matter. The recordings of the MILLSTONE and BELLSTONE are missing or
incomplete, and every recording is a little "off-key". The CONDUCTOR can fix
both only with the slivers in the player's BADGE case. That is why TEAM
DISTORTION lets the player keep winning ("Let them keep collecting...").

## The cast

**{PLAYER}.** A new trainer from Willowbrook. Quiet, stubborn, always on time.

**MOM.** Warm and funny; she patches up the team whenever you white out at home.

**PROF. LINDEN.** Willowbrook's AIMON professor. He gives out the starters,
explains evolution, and later works out what the BADGES really are.

**KAI.** The rival next door, spiky-haired, loud and always one step ahead
("Beat you here!"). His older brother **RYKER** left for the AIMON LEAGUE eight
years ago and never came home. Kai wants to become CHAMPION so that wherever
Ryker is, he'll hear about it. Kai loses to TEAM DISTORTION early (VESPER) and
spends the story getting stronger. He ends it as CHAMPION for exactly ten
minutes, until you arrive.

**The HISTORIAN of Archford.** Keeper of old texts. He tells the legend of the
RIFT and the CHAMPION'S OATH, and wonders "who else has been reading my
books".

**The WARDENS (GYM LEADERS).**
- **HOLT** (Grayhaven, NORMAL). A stern stonemason. His family guarded the RIFTSTONE for generations. He calls the WARDENS together.
- **IVY** (Cedarwood, GRASS). A gentle gardener with a fierce streak. She tore the RESONATOR off the ROOTSTONE.
- **NERISSA** (Seabreeze, WATER). "The CAPTAIN." She sails the player to the islands in the endgame.
- **TOR** (Cragmoor, ROCK). A quarry boss who chased TEAM DISTORTION off with a shovel.
- **SAHRA** (Sunspire, GROUND). A sharp-tongued archaeologist who has spent her life digging up the FIRST SCORE.
- **WREN** (Meadowfield, ELECTRIC). A young farmer-inventor who powers the farm with wind and VOLTVIX. SONANCE ENERGY keeps trying to buy her windmill.
- **CANTOR** (Stonepeak, SOUND). The oldest WARDEN: a composer and bell-keeper. Long ago, AUGUST VALE was his brightest pupil.
- **NOX** (Starfall Isle, DARK). A night-sky astronomer. "Stars need the dark to be seen."

**TEAM DISTORTION.** Black coats with violet trim and a broken-wave emblem.
Their front is **SONANCE ENERGY**, the power company that keeps Silverfall's
lights on.
- **The CONDUCTOR: DR. AUGUST VALE.** Founder of SONANCE ENERGY, a beloved philanthropist and a cellist. Years ago his partner AIMON, a SONARION named **ECHO**, fell silent and died. Vale came to believe the old texts: that the RIFT cut VALEMORA's song in half, and that beyond it every silenced voice still sings. He is not cruel. He is sure he is right, and that makes him dangerous.
- **The COMMANDER: RYKER, Kai's older brother.** He won all eight BADGES and then lost the CHAMPION match. "Second place is silence." Vale promised him a world where only the strong are heard. He carries the only key card to the SONANCE TOWER's 4th floor, and nobody has ever taken anything from him.
- **ADMIN VESPER** (GHOST and SOUND). Poetic, curious, the first to find the player "interesting". Reading the FIRST SCORE makes her doubt, and she defects before the end.
- **ADMIN THANE** (ELECTRIC). A storm-bringer and brawler, loyal and loud. He is arrested at Meadowfield.
- **ADMIN MORROW** (GHOST and DARK). The keeper of the archive, who turns a small hourglass in his hand. He always escapes through a warp pad ("Time is on my side"). He is loyal to the very end, then vanishes.
- **GRUNTS, and a SCIENTIST who wants out.**

**The ELITE FOUR and the old CHAMPION.**
- **The old CHAMPION ORLA.** The trainer who beat Ryker eight years ago. Kai beats her just before you reach the top.
- **ELITE FOUR: SHADE** (GHOST), **BRAWN** (FIGHTING and NORMAL), **CINDRA** (FIRE) and **MARINA** (WATER and FLYING).

## PROJECT COUNTERMELODY (TEAM DISTORTION's plan)

1. **Record.** Clamp a RESONATOR to each KEYSTONE and record its song. Cracking a stone (RIFTSTONE) makes the recording louder.
2. **Tune.** Store the songs in the SONANCE archive (3F). For months, the GRAND RESONATOR (4F) sends a faint tuning signal down every SONANCE power line in the region, weakening the seals.
3. **Complete.** Fill the gaps and fix the "off-key" notes using the slivers in a full BADGE case. A trainer with eight BADGES is a walking copy of the whole chord.
4. **Perform.** At the SUNKEN SHRINE, where the RIFT was first sealed, play all eight songs backwards at once. The seals come undone and the RIFT opens.
